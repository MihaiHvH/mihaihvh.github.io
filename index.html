About Me

I’m a computer science student at the University of Bucharest, Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, where I started in October 2026. I’m self-taught and have been writing C++ for 8 years, and I’m always learning something new. I compete in cybersecurity and informatics contests, and I took part in the national round of the National Cybersecurity Olympiad in 2026.

Away from the screen I ride bikes, mountain bikes especially, and I know how to repair them. I like being in nature. In my free time I 3D print custom models for people close to me and engrave messages in wood with the same machine.

Skills: C++, Python, x86 assembly, web, network, mobile and pwn security, Linux (Fedora, Kali), Windows, Arduino and ESP32, 3D modeling (FreeCAD, Tinkercad), 3D printing (Ideamaker)

Education: Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, University of Bucharest (2026 to present); Colegiul Național de Informatică, Piatra Neamț (2022 to 2026); Cybersecurity course, Algolymp (2025 to 2026); informatics qualification at the “Hai la Olimpiadă” Center of Excellence (2018 to 2026)

Volunteering: Cozla Bike, supervising participants at Marea Mișcare and Gârcina Bike Tour (2023 to present); team coordination at the Team Informatics Olympiad national stage and PROSOFT@NT (2024); school days activities (2023); organizer of the “Ziua Științelor” symposium (2025)

Languages: Romanian (native), English (B2)
